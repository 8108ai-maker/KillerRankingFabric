## 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: pixel-art combat VFX animation sprite sheet for a Minecraft weapon swing trail, transparent RGBA PNG. Create EXACTLY 8 animation frames arranged in a regular 4-column by 2-row grid on a square canvas, equal cell sizes, in reading order. Each cell contains the SAME horizontal ribbon/slash oriented left-to-right, positioned consistently and with generous transparent padding so no frame touches another cell. Flat 2D sharp square pixel art, low resolution look, white bright core with pale warm ivory edges, three or four flat tones, stepped angular silhouette, no smooth gradients. This sprite is stretched over the actual curved swept path of a bat, knife or axe in a game: keep each sprite a nearly straight thin horizontal band, bright narrow leading tip on the RIGHT and tapered trailing left edge, NOT a crescent or a whole circular arc. Frame 1 (top left): short initial bright slash near the right half. Frame 2: band opens to full width, thick central highlight. Frame 3: full-width strongest white stroke with two smaller parallel streaks. Frame 4: full-width ivory stroke starts breaking into connected pixel streaks. Frame 5: thinner, more gaps in the band. Frame 6: thinner broken streaks. Frame 7: sparse short fading fragments along the same band. Frame 8 (bottom right): very sparse tiny dim ivory remnants, nearly gone. The effect should visibly change shape over the 8 frames rather than merely change opacity. No isolated confetti field, no colored particles, no sword, no weapon, no character, no ground, no UI, no lettering, no numbers, no grid lines, no panel frames, no checkerboard painted background. Actual transparent background.
